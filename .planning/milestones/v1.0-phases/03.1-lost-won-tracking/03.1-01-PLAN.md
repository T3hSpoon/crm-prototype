<objective>
**As a** salesperson working the pipeline, **I want to** mark a deal as lost only after giving a reason, **so that** every deal that falls through carries data on why, instead of silently vanishing from the active pipeline with no trace of what happened.

This plan delivers LOST-01 and LOST-02 as one vertical slice: Task 1 (tracer) wires the entire gated-commit path end to end — a new Popover primitive, the atomic `moveToLost` store action, and `StageSelect`'s intercept of the "Lost" selection — proving the architecture with just the required Reason category. Task 2 (expansion) adds the optional free-text Note, a new Textarea primitive, and the failure-resilience/error-copy the UI-SPEC's design contract requires.

Purpose: closes the Lost half of Phase 3.1 and unblocks Phase 4's FCST-02 (loss-reason breakdown), which needs real `lostReason` data to exist.
Output: picking "Lost" in any row's stage dropdown always opens a reason gate; a deal only ever enters the Lost group carrying a selected reason (and optional note); dismissing the gate leaves the deal exactly where it was.
</objective>
