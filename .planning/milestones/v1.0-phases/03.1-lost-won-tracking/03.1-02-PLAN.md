<objective>
**As a** salesperson closing a deal, **I want to** capture its contract terms the moment I mark it Won, **so that** the deal only lands on the "contracts made so far" list once real terms are on record, instead of the list being just another undifferentiated pipeline stage.

This plan delivers PIPE-03 and WON-01 as one vertical slice, depending on 03.1-01-PLAN.md (both plans touch `StageSelect.tsx`, `pipelineStore.ts`, and `deal.ts`). Task 1 (tracer) registers "won" as a genuinely distinct `PipelineGroup` end to end — data model, derivation, group registration, repository allowlist, the atomic `moveToWon` action, the new contract-terms dialog, and `StageSelect`'s intercept — all in one pass, because a new `PipelineGroup` member is unusable (and crashes at render) until every one of those places knows about it; there is no smaller working slice. Task 2 (expansion) adds failure resilience, the "Contracts"-specific empty-state copy, and seed data that demonstrates the Won state on first load.

Purpose: closes the Won half of Phase 3.1, completes PIPE-03's "contracts made so far" framing, and unblocks Phase 4's forecast page (which needs real won/lost data).
Output: picking "Won" in any row's stage dropdown opens a contract-terms form; a deal only ever enters the Contracts group carrying start/end/signed dates and payment terms; the Contracts group is visually and behaviorally distinct from the active Deal stage.
</objective>
